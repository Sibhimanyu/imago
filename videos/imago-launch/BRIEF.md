## Intent

A SaaS-style, upbeat launch video for Imago (imago.onslate.in). The story is
before → after: a GET URL is typed, a wall of raw JSON scrolls, then on
beat-synced cuts it resolves into real Imago interfaces — Pokémon profile with
stat bars, weather dashboard with a chart, book table. Three kinetic-type
proof beats: "The model never writes HTML" · "Remembers every shape" ·
"Watches endpoints change". Close on the Imago lockup, "APIs become
interfaces.", imago.onslate.in.

The mascot Amigo leads a bit (user, mid-brief: "I also want the mascot to lead
a bit"): Amigo opens the video, guides the eye to the JSON, reacts to the
transformation, and shares the end card with the logo.

## Notes

- v2 (2026-09-25): user feedback on v1: "very boring… better music… more character to our mascot… jam in more content in the same duration." v2 = 9 frames, cuts every 1–2 bars, a 128 bpm drop track, real app screenshots, and an Amigo character bible (motion-only personality).

- Brand rules (docs/BRAND-SYSTEM.md): ink + warm paper, no brand hue; colour only for meaning
  (amber = changed, green = live). Inter/system sans, JetBrains Mono for machine text.
- Amigo: single colour, no face, arms, speech bubbles or expressions — personality comes
  from motion (hop, lean, squash) and surrounding copy only.
- Never use Amigo as the main logo; the end card's logo is the Imago mark + wordmark.
